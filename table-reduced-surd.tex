\section{Reduced Set of Revival Parameters}

\begin{observation}[Interchangeability of $k$ and $m$]
The results are symmetric under the exchange $L_x \leftrightarrow L_y$:
every parameter set $(\rho_x,\delta_x,\rho_y,\delta_y)$ that yields a
revival of period $N$ on $L_x \times L_y$ also yields a revival of the same
period $N$ on $L_y \times L_x$ with $(\rho_x,\delta_x)$ and
$(\rho_y,\delta_y)$ interchanged. This follows from the symmetry of the
separable coin $C = C_x \otimes C_y$ and the commutativity of the two shift
operators. Consequently, only one orientation of each non-square torus
needs to be tabulated; the $m \times k$ entries are obtained by swapping
the $x$ and $y$ columns. The table below lists the reduced set.
\end{observation}
