The Bell states are maximally entangled two-qubit states. In terms of
the four spatial directions:
\begin{align}
  |\Phi^+\rangle &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|R\rangle + |D\rangle), \\
  |\Phi^-\rangle &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|R\rangle - |D\rangle), \\
  |\Psi^+\rangle &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|L\rangle + |U\rangle), \\
  |\Psi^-\rangle &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|L\rangle - |U\rangle).
\end{align}
