\par
The reflection parameters $\rho_x, \rho_y$ in the reduced
classification take a small set of algebraic values. Every value
is either a rational of low denominator ($1/2$, $1/3$, $1/4$,
$2/3$, $3/4$) or a quadratic surd of the form $(a \pm \sqrt{b})/c$
with $b \in \{2, 3, 5\}$. The surd families split into three
groups: the $\sqrt{2}$-family associated with eighth roots of
unity, the $\sqrt{3}$-family associated with twelfth roots, and
the $\sqrt{5}$-family associated with fifth roots. Each family
arises from a specific cycle length: $k = 4$ contributes the
$\sqrt{2}$-values, $k = 3$ and $k = 6$ the $\sqrt{3}$-values,
and $k = 5$ and $k = 10$ the pentagonal values
$(5 \pm \sqrt{5})/10$.
