Extending the classification framework established by Dukes
\cite{Dukes2014} for 1D cycle graphs, we provide the first complete
classification of 2D separable discrete-time quantum walks
on $k \times m$ toroidal lattices. We observe that separable coins
produce a rich set of intrinsic full-state revivals on this topology, given the
rarity of these in other configurations.

Prior to the present work, 2D revivals was less explored
than the 1D case. Full state revivals of period two ($N=2$) were known for 2D quantum
walks on a plane, and it was originally proved that no longer cycles
exist for the general four-state walk \cite{Stefanak2010}. 
Subsequently, Dodangodage used the most general form of characteristic polynomial
to construct counterexamples with longer-period revivals \cite{Dodangodage2018}. 
Independently, Jayakody demonstrated
that coin operators can be designed to produce full state
revivals of any desired period in higher spatial dimensions
\cite{Jayakody2018}. 

This work classifies which revival periods arise for the natural family of separable coins on toroidal lattices.
Throughout this paper, the word \textit{revival} refers to a full-state revival.
