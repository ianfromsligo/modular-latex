\par
Extending the framework of Dukes \cite{Dukes2014} for
1D cycle graphs, we provide a complete classification of
separable discrete-time quantum walks on $k \times m$
toroidal lattices, for $k, m \le 16$ and $N \le 60$. The
classification is built in three steps: a 1D search over the
coin parameter space, a reduction that collapses harmonics
and symmetry-equivalent entries, and a 2D extension via the
tensor factorisation $U_{k \times m} = U_k \otimes U_m$.

Prior to the present work, 2D revivals were less explored
than the 1D case. Full-state revivals of period two ($N = 2$)
were known for 2D quantum walks on a plane, and it was
originally proved that no longer cycles exist for the general
four-state walk \cite{Stefanak2010}. Subsequently,
Dodangodage used the most general form of the characteristic
polynomial to construct counterexamples with longer-period
revivals \cite{Dodangodage2018}. Independently, Jayakody
demonstrated that coin operators can be designed to produce
full-state revivals of any desired period in higher spatial
dimensions \cite{Jayakody2018}. Our work differs in that we
classify which revival periods arise for a *natural* family
of coins---Dukes' separable parametrisation---rather than
constructing coins to order.
