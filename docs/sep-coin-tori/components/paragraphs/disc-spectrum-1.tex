The five most populated periods are $N=54$ ($659$ sets), $N=58$ ($361$ sets), $N=30$ ($332$ sets), $N=50$ ($275$ sets), $N=24$ ($263$ sets). These are
multiples of many small integers, so several 1D cycles share the
period and the 2D cross-product is large. The sparsest periods are
$N=2$ ($1$), $N=4$ ($16$), $N=28$ ($23$), $N=42$ ($25$), $N=32$ ($44$); these admit 1D revivals on only a small subset of
cycles. The polarisation between dense and sparse periods is a
direct reflection of the arithmetic of the 1D revival sets.
