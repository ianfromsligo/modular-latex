The four-state walk on a 2D lattice admits $N = 2$ revivals but no
longer cycles within the class considered by \v{S}tefa\v{n}\'ak et al.\
\cite{Stefanak2010}. Their coin is non-separable, as are the coins in
the later constructions of Dodangodage \cite{Dodangodage2018} and
Jayakody \cite{Jayakody2018}. Dodangodage constructed walks with
revivals of longer period; Jayakody showed that a non-separable coin can
be designed to produce revivals of any desired period. Our
classification covers the separable case, where the revival structure
follows from the two 1D factors rather than from a single non-separable
coin.
