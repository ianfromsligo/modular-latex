\subsection{2D Classification}
The results of the tensor factorisation show that:
$28$ tori revive, at $59$ distinct
periods, with $18{,}174$ distinct parameter sets in total.
The distribution is highly uneven. The largest families
occur on the tori $4 \times 4$, $3 \times 4$, and
$3 \times 3$, which together account for just under half the
classification. These are the cycles with the densest 1D
spectra: the $3$-cycle carries 41 signatures and the
$4$-cycle 69, so their tensor products
generate many distinct parameter combinations. The tori
involving $k = 2$ are the sparsest, because the $2$-cycle
carries only three signatures, at $N_{\min} \in \{2, 24, 24\}$.

Note the 1D grid search stops at $N \le 60$, while the 2D
classification extends to $N \le 420$. 
The 2D enumeration is built from the catalogued 1D
signatures, whose periods are all at most $60$, and a 2D period is
either one of those periods or a combination of a pair of them.
The 2D scan therefore inherits the 1D catalogue rather than
re-searching 1D at larger $N$; periods above $60$ arise only as
combinations of 1D revival periods.
