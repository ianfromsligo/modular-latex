\emph{1D catalogue.}
The catalogue contains $172$ signatures across the seven cycle
lengths $k \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$, transcribed from Dukes'
tables together with the $k = 2$ correction. Every signature is
verified at the operator level, and the verification pass is
reported in Appendix, Table ~\ref{tab:1d-catalogue-exact}.

\emph{1D exhaustive grid search.}
On a $1001 \times 360$ grid over $(\rho, \delta)$, for $k \le 16$
and $N \le 60$, the exhaustive search finds no exact revival for
$k \notin \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$ outside the degenerate families
at $\rho \in \{0, 1\}$ and $\delta \in \{0, \pi\}$. Every candidate
recorded by the grid search is confirmed by a matrix-power check at
its exact period.  

\emph{2D enumeration.}
The 2D stage, built from the tensor factorisation, produces
revivals on $28$ distinct tori, at $59$ distinct periods, and with
a total of $18{,}174$ distinct parameter sets. Each parameter set is
recorded once, at its smallest exact revival period; the
classification is available as supplementary data.
