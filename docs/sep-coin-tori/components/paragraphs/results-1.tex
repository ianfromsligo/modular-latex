\emph{Demonstration.}
Table~\ref{tab:k4-demo} shows the step-by-step amplitudes at the
projective and exact revival steps for this $k = 4$ case. An exact
revival occurs only at $N = 24$; the revivals at $N = 6, 12, 18$
are projective.

\emph{1D catalogue.}
The catalogue contains $172$ signatures across the seven cycle
lengths $k \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$, transcribed from Dukes'
tables together with the $k = 2$ correction. Every signature is
verified at the operator level, and the verification pass is
reported in Appendix~\ref{app:1d-verification}.

\emph{1D exhaustive grid search.}
On a $1001 \times 360$ grid over $(\rho, \delta)$, for $k \le 16$
and $N \le 60$, the exhaustive search finds no exact revival for
$k \notin \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$ outside the degenerate families
at $\rho \in \{0, 1\}$ and $\delta \in \{0, \pi\}$. Every candidate
recorded by the grid search is confirmed by a matrix-power check at
its exact period. The worst eigenvalue residual over the grid is
$2.0$, the maximum possible value for $|\lambda_j^N - 1|$ with
$|\lambda_j| = 1$, confirming that the null result is not a
numerical artefact.

\emph{2D enumeration.}
The 2D stage, built from the tensor factorisation, produces
revivals on $28$ distinct tori, at $59$ distinct periods, and with
a total of $18{,}174$ reduced parameter sets. The reduced
classification records each parameter set once, at its smallest
exact revival period; the full list records every period at which
each set revives, and contains $103{,}427$ entries. Both are
available as supplementary data. Table~\ref{tab:tori-summary}
lists the $28$ tori with their record counts and period ranges.
