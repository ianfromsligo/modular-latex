\par
The search produces a classification of full-state revivals
across $\Kmax$ cycle lengths and $\Nmax$-step revival
periods. The 1D stage finds revivals for exactly seven cycle
lengths, $k \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$, and none for
$k \in \{7, 9, 11, 12, 16\}$. The 2D stage, built from the
tensor factorisation, produces revivals on $24$ distinct
tori, at $30$ distinct periods (every even $N \le 60$), and
with a total of $4{,}773$ canonical parameter sets after
collapsing continuous families. Of these, $1{,}100$ have
$\delta_x = \delta_y = 0$; the remaining $3{,}673$ involve
at least one non-zero phase. Table~\ref{tab:tori-summary}
records the $24$ tori with their set counts and period
ranges.
