\par
The distribution across tori is also uneven. The tori $2\times2$ ($1196$ sets), $2\times4$ ($544$ sets), $2\times3$ ($500$ sets), $2\times6$ ($427$ sets), $4\times4$ ($426$ sets)
account for the majority of the classification. This concentration
is a direct consequence of the 1D structure: $k = 2$ admits 1D
revivals at every even period, so any torus involving a $2$-cycle
inherits a large family of 2D revivals. Conversely, the prime
cycles $k = 5$ and $k = 10$ revive only at $N = 60$, so tori
involving them are restricted to a single long period.
