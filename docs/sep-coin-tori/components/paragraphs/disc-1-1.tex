The period spectrum is equally uneven. Of the $30$ distinct
periods, the majority are populated by only a handful of
parameter sets; the peak periods are $N = 24$ and $N = 12$,
which together account for a large fraction of the
classification. These values are highly composite, so
multiple 1D cycles share them, and the resulting 2D
cross-products are large. At the other extreme, the periods
$N = 2$ (on the $2 \times 2$ torus only) and $N = 60$ (on
the pentagonal tori) are isolated.
