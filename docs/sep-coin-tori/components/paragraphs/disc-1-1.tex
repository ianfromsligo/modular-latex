The period spectrum is also scattered. Of the $59$ distinct
periods, the majority are populated by only a handful of
parameter sets. The peak periods are $N = 60$ with $2{,}456$
reduced parameter sets, $N = 120$ with $1{,}642$, $N = 420$ with
$1{,}076$, $N = 24$ with $719$, and $N = 30$ with $581$. Each is
highly composite and hence an LCM of many pairs of 1D revival
periods, so a large number of 2D combinations share it. At the
other extreme, the periods $N = 2$ and $N = 6$ are isolated on
one torus and on three tori respectively.
