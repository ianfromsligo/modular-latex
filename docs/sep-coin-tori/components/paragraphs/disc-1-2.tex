\par
Square tori $L \times L$ admit revivals for every $L$ in the
classification: $2\times2$, $3\times3$, $4\times4$, $5\times5$, $6\times6$, $8\times8$, $10\times10$. Non-square tori revive
precisely when their 1D revival-period sets intersect, and the
reduced classification lists only one orientation of each such
pair. Revivals on non-square tori are typically asymmetric
($\rho_x \neq \rho_y$), whereas square tori frequently admit
both symmetric and asymmetric realisations.
