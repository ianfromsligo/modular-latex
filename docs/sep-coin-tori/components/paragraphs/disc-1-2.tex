\par
The reflection parameters are algebraic. Every observed
$\rho$ is either a low-denominator rational ($1/2$, $1/3$,
$1/4$, $2/3$, $3/4$) or a quadratic surd of the form
$(a \pm \sqrt{b})/c$ with $b \in \{2, 3, 5\}$. The
surds split into three families: the $\sqrt{2}$-family
associated with eighth roots of unity (from $k = 4$), the
$\sqrt{3}$-family associated with twelfth roots (from
$k = 3$ and $k = 6$), and the pentagonal family
$(5 \pm \sqrt{5})/10$ (from $k = 5$ and $k = 10$). The
duality $\rho \leftrightarrow 1 - \rho$ holds without
exception, so each value is paired with its complement
elsewhere in the classification.
