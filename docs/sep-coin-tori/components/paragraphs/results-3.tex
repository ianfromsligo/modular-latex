Table~\ref{tab:maxN-rho} gives, for each torus, a
representative parameter set at its maximum revival period,
with the reflection parameters in exact form. These are the
longest-period revivals on each torus, and they provide a
concrete anchor for the classification: the $5 \times 5$
torus revives at $N = 60$ with $\rho = (5-\sqrt{5})/10$, a
golden-ratio surd; the $8 \times 8$ torus revives at
$N = 24$ at the Hadamard point $\rho = 1/2$; and the
$3 \times 3$ torus revives at $N = 30$ with
$\rho = (5-\sqrt{5})/6$.
