The $59$ revival periods range from $N = 2$ to the scan bound
$N = 420$. The largest counts occur at the highly composite
periods, notably $N = 60$ with $2{,}456$ records, $N = 120$ with
$1{,}642$, $N = 420$ with $1{,}076$, $N = 24$ with $719$, and
$N = 30$ with $581$. These values are the lcms of many pairs of
1D revival periods and therefore support many parameter
combinations. The smallest periods are sparse: $N = 2$ appears
only on the $2 \times 2$ torus at $\delta_x = \delta_y = 0$, while
$N = 6, 8, 10$ appear on a handful of tori each.
