We have presented a complete classification of full-state revivals
for separable discrete-time quantum walks on $k \times m$ toroidal
lattices, within the search bound $k, m \le \Kmax$ and $N \le \Nmax$.
A 2D torus exhibits full-state revivals whenever both spatial
dimensions belong to the 1D revival set and share a common revival
period, and no revival exists outside this class within the bound.
The experimental results confirm this classification and reveal three
novel phenomena: multi-period revivals, mixed-$\rho$ parameters, and
mixed-$\delta$ asymmetry. The longest revival period $N=60$ occurs
on the small $5\times5$ torus, inverting the classical
intuition. These results open new directions for quantum state
routing and quantum memory architectures.
