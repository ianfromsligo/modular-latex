\par
The phase parameters $\delta_x, \delta_y$ take only rational
multiples of $\pi$, with denominators dividing the revival period
or twice the torus dimension. Zero phase is by far the most
common. Non-zero phases cluster around values whose denominators
match the underlying 1D cycle: $2\pi/5$ and $4\pi/5$ on the
pentagonal tori, $2\pi/3$ and $4\pi/3$ on the $3$- and $6$-cycles.
The role of $\delta$ is to select, among the family of revivals
at a given $(\rho_x, \rho_y)$, those phase combinations that
satisfy the root-of-unity condition on both axes simultaneously.
