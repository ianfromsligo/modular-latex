Table~\ref{tab:delta-summary} records the distribution of
the phase parameters. Contrary to the 1D case, where
$\delta$ does not affect the revival period, the phase
parameters are decisive in 2D: only $23.0\%$ of the
classification has $\delta_x = \delta_y = 0$, a further
$12.5\%$ has $\delta_x = \delta_y \neq 0$, and the
remaining $64.4\%$ requires unequal phases on the two axes.
The appearing phases are always rational multiples of $\pi$
with denominators dividing twice the cycle length. The full
1D signature catalogue is available in the repository as a
machine-readable dataset.
