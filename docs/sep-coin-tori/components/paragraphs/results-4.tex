\emph{Phase selection.}
Table~\ref{tab:delta-summary} records the distribution of the
phase parameters across the $18{,}174$ reduced revivals. In 1D,
the phase $\delta$ selects which $(\rho, \delta)$ pairs lie in the
catalogue but does not change the period at which a given $\rho$
revives, once it is in the catalogue; the sole exception is the
$k = 2$ case, where $\rho = 1/2$ revives at $N = 2$ for
$\delta = 0$ and at $N = 24$ for $\delta = \pi/2$ or $3\pi/2$. In
2D, by contrast, $\delta$ becomes a selection mechanism in its own
right. Only $25.9\%$ of the classification has
$\delta_x = \delta_y = 0$. A further $48.9\%$ has zero phase on
exactly one axis (either $\delta_x = 0$ or $\delta_y = 0$, but not
both), $5.0\%$ has $\delta_x = \delta_y \neq 0$, and the remaining
$20.2\%$ has unequal non-zero phases on the two axes. All appearing
phases are rational multiples of $\pi$ with denominators dividing
$2k$. The full 1D signature catalogue is given in
Table~\ref{tab:1d-catalogue-exact} in the Appendix.
