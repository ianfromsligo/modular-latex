The classification is built and verified in two notebooks,
\texttt{EXP033.ipynb} and \texttt{EXP032b.ipynb}, available in the
accompanying repository \cite{full_revivals_references}. The method
proceeds in four stages.

\emph{Revival demonstration (EXP033).} We evolve the walk step by step for
$k = 4$, $\rho = 1/2$, $\delta = \pi/2$ and record the state at each
step. The walker returns to its initial position and spin at
$N \in \{6,12,18,24\}$; these four revivals carry different global
phases, three projective and one exact. This demonstrates the
distinction between the two criteria on a single worked example.

\emph{1D verification (EXP033).} We transcribe the complete set of 1D revival
solutions tabulated by Dukes for cycle lengths
$k \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$, together with the $k = 2$,
$\rho = 1/2$, $\delta \in \{\pi/2, 3\pi/2\}$ correction, and verify
each entry at the operator level using the eigenvalue residual
\begin{equation}
r(k, \rho, \delta, N)
= \max_j \left| \lambda_j^N - \overline{\lambda^N} \right| ,
\label{eq:eig-residual}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_j$ are the eigenvalues of $U_k(\rho, \delta)$ and
$\overline{\lambda^N}$ is their $N$-th power mean. The residual
vanishes when $U^N = e^{i\phi} I$; the exact case $U^N = I$ is
confirmed separately by a matrix-power check at the recorded period.

\emph{Exhaustive grid search (EXP032b).} We search for exact
revivals on 1D cycles for $k \le 16$ by sweeping a dense grid over
$(\rho, \delta)$ and testing $U^N = I$ up to $N = 60$ at each grid
point. The operator is diagonalised once per grid point, and the
exact eigenvalue residual $\max_j |\lambda_j^N - 1|$ is evaluated
for every $N$. Grid points that pass the tolerance are recorded as
\emph{candidate revivals}. Each candidate is then refined: its
parameters are snapped to the nearest algebraic value, the exact
period is confirmed by a direct matrix-power check
$\|U^N - I\| < 10^{-10}$, and the confirmed revival is classified
as trivial, Dukes-tabulated, or new. Trivial revivals are those at
the degenerate parameter values $\rho \in \{0, 1\}$ or
$\delta \in \{0, \pi\}$.

\emph{2D enumeration (EXP033).} For a separable walk on a $k \times m$ torus,
the single-step operator factorises as
$U_{k \times m} = U_k \otimes U_m$. We enumerate all ordered pairs
$(k, m)$ with $k, m \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$ and $k \le m$,
every pair of 1D signatures on those cycles, and every $N$ from $1$
to $420$. For each pair the operator is diagonalised once and the
projective residual is evaluated at every $N$; the exact period is
derived from the phase order via
$N_{\text{exact}} = N_{\text{proj}} \cdot \mathrm{ord}(\phi)$,
where $\mathrm{ord}(\phi)$ is the smallest $r$ with $\phi^r = 1$.
Scanning every $N$ rather than only multiples of the factor lcm is
necessary because the phase-cancellation case can revive exactly at
a proper divisor of the lcm. Each canonical record is confirmed by a
matrix-power check $\|U^N - I\| < 10^{-10}$ at its exact period.
