The spectrum splits into three regimes. The short-period regime
$N \le 12$ carries $579$ sets on a small number
of symmetric tori. The intermediate regime $14 \le N \le 36$
carries $1981$ sets, dominated by cross-products of
small cycles. The long-period regime $N \ge 38$ carries
$2213$ sets, spread over the widest range of
reflection and phase parameters. The longest period, $N = 60$,
occurs only on the $5 \times 5$, $5 \times 10$, and
$10 \times 10$ tori, all inherited from the pentagonal cycles
$k = 5$ and $k = 10$.
