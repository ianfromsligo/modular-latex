\par
The spectrum can be divided into three regimes. The short-period
regime $N \le 12$ carries $579$ parameter sets
across a small number of highly symmetric tori. The intermediate
regime $14 \le N \le 36$ carries $1981$ sets,
dominated by the cross-products of the small cycles. The long-period
regime $N \ge 38$ carries $2213$ sets, and although
these periods appear on fewer tori, they exhibit the widest range
of reflection and phase parameters. The longest period, $N = 60$,
is concentrated entirely on the $5 \times 5$, $5 \times 10$, and
$10 \times 10$ tori, all of which inherit their revival structure
from the pentagonal cycles $k = 5$ and $k = 10$.
