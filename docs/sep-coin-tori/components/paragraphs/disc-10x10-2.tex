Figure~\ref{fig:10x10-fidelity} tracks the fidelity of the walker
against the initial state over eighty steps. The fidelity reaches
its maximum at $N = 60$ with a value numerically indistinguishable
from $1$; no other step in the range produces a full-state revival.
Figure~\ref{fig:10x10-probability} shows the position probability
distribution at ten snapshots. The walker starts localised at the
origin, spreads outward over the first thirty steps, and refocuses
to the origin at $N = 60$, matching the initial condition almost
exactly.
