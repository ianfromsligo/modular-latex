\par\section*{Use of AI-assisted tools}
\label{sec:ai-disclosure}
The core mathematics and analysis programs for the 1D cycle and 2D
torus classifications were developed by the authors. Once that
foundation was trusted and verified, the authors used DeepSeek (chat interface;
model version not exposed at time of use - Aug-Oct 2026) to refine and 
augment the work into the final versions  of the  notebooks, \texttt{EXP032b.ipynb}, 
\texttt{EXP033.ipynb}. 
The AI assisted in three further areas: developing theorems: the cyclotomic-field
sketch of Conjecture~\ref{conj:no-other-k}; migrating the numerical
results from the notebook outputs into the LaTeX tables; and producing the 
fidelity and distribution plots that accompany the notebook. 
It was used to review drafts of the manuscript text for correctness.

The AI-assisted output was checked at each stage. The notebook
verifies every 1D signature and every 2D record against an
operator-level eigenvalue residual, and the results were compared
with the tabulated values of Dukes \cite{Dukes2014}. All tables were
regenerated from the notebook. The authors reviewed the AI-assisted output
and checked the tables against the classification data.
They take full responsibility for the accuracy,
originality, and integrity of the manuscript.
