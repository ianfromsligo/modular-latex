\par\section*{Use of AI-assisted tools}
\label{sec:ai-disclosure}
The authors used DeepSeek-V4-Pro (DeepSeek, 2026) during the preparation of this manuscript, specifically for drafting and refining the proofs of Theorems 1 and 2, for the sketch of Conjecture 3, for the design and implementation of the Python analysis pipeline described in Sec. III, and for LaTeX formatting and bibliographic assistance. All mathematical derivations and all code produced with AI assistance were independently verified by the authors: the proofs by direct matrix exponentiation of the walk operators and comparison with the tabulated results of Dukes [3]; the code by operator-level residual checks, by reproduction of every 1D signature and every 2D record, and by numerical evaluation of the root-of-unity residual at the accepted revival parameters. The authors reviewed and edited all AI-assisted content and take full responsibility for the accuracy, originality, and integrity of the manuscript.
