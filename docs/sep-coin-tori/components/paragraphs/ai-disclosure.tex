\par\section*{Use of AI-assisted tools}
\label{sec:ai-disclosure}
The authors used an AI-assisted large language model during the
preparation of this manuscript, specifically for drafting and
refining the proofs of Theorems~\ref{thm:1d-existence} and
\ref{thm:1d-nonexistence}, for the sketch of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:no-other-k}, and for LaTeX formatting and
bibliographic assistance. All mathematical derivations produced
with AI assistance were independently verified by the authors
using direct matrix exponentiation of the walk operators, by
comparison with the tabulated results of Dukes
\cite{Dukes2014}, and by numerical evaluation of the
root-of-unity residual at the accepted revival parameters. The
authors reviewed and edited all AI-assisted content and take
full responsibility for the accuracy, originality, and integrity
of the manuscript.
