\par
Square and non-square tori differ in their symmetry structure.
Square tori admit symmetric realisations ($\rho_x = \rho_y$) and
asymmetric ones, with the symmetric family generally contributing
the largest block of parameter sets. On non-square tori, the two
cycle lengths must share a revival period, and the reflection
values on the two axes must be tuned separately. This constraint
is what distinguishes the non-square tori structurally.
