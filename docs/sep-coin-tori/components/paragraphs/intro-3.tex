Dukes \cite{Dukes2014} classified full-state revivals on 1D cycle
graphs. Building on this work, we extend the classification to
separable DTQWs over 2D toroidal lattices. A
separable coin factorises into independent 1D coins on each axis of a
$k \times m$ torus. The two factors revive independently, and the
torus walk revives when they both do. The classification covers the admissible 
cycle lengths $k, m \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$ with revival periods up
to $N = 420$.
