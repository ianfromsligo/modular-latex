Dukes classified full-state revivals on 1D cycle graphs
\cite{Dukes2014}. We extend that classification to separable
discrete-time quantum walks: walks whose coin factorises into
independent 1D coins on each axis on $k \times m$ toroidal
lattices, within the bounds $k, m \le 16$ and $N \le 60$. The
classification proceeds in three stages: a systematic search of
the 1D coin parameter space, a reduction step that removes
redundant entries, and a 2D extension via tensor factorisation
of the evolution into two 1D walks.
