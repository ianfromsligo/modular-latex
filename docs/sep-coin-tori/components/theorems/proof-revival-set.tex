\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:1d-existence}]
Existence is established by explicit witnesses. For
$k \in \{2,3,4,6\}$, infinite families of solutions are given in
Dukes~\cite{Dukes2014}, \S4.2--4.4. For $k \in \{5,8,10\}$, Dukes
provides finite sets of explicit triples $(\rho, \delta, N)$ in \S4.5.
We reproduce one representative triple for each admissible $k$ in
Table~\ref{tab:1d-witnesses}. For each row, the eigenphases follow from
the dispersion relation
$\sin\theta_j = \sqrt{\rho}\,\sin(2\pi j / k)$
of Eq.~\eqref{eq:disp_rel}; substituting the stated $(\rho, N)$ and
checking that every eigenphase is a rational multiple of $\pi/N$
confirms the revival condition $U_k^N = e^{i\phi} I_{2k}$ directly.
