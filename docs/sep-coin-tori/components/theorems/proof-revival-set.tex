\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:1d-existence}]
Existence is established by explicit witnesses. For
$k \in \{2, 3, 4, 6\}$ infinite families of solutions
are given by Dukes \cite{Dukes2014}, \S4.2--4.4; for
$k \in \{5, 8, 10\}$ Dukes provides finite sets of
explicit triples $(\rho, \delta, N)$ in \S4.5. Table~
\ref{tab:1d-catalogue-exact} reproduces one representative per
admissible $k$. The eigenphases follow from the dispersion
relation $\sin\theta_j = \sqrt{\rho}\sin(2\pi j/k)$;
substituting each stated $(\rho, N)$ and checking that
every eigenphase is a rational multiple of $\pi/N$ confirms
the revival condition $U_k^N = e^{i\phi} I_{2k}$ directly.
\end{proof}
