\begin{corollary}[2D existence]
\label{cor:2d-existence}
A separable quantum walk on a $k \times m$ toroidal lattice exhibits a
full-state revival whenever both spatial dimensions belong to the 1D
revival set $k, m \in \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$ and their revival
period sets share a common element,
$\mathcal{N}_k \cap \mathcal{N}_m \neq \emptyset$.
\end{corollary}
