\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:1d-nonexistence}]
We performed a grid search over the parameter space
$\rho \in \{\, j \cdot 10^{-4} : j = 1, \ldots, 9999 \,\}$,
$\delta/\pi$ on the twenty rational values
$\{0, \frac{1}{6}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{2}{3},
\frac{3}{4}, \frac{5}{6}, 1, \frac{2}{5}, \frac{4}{5}, \frac{6}{5},
\frac{8}{5}, \frac{5}{4}, \frac{4}{3}, \frac{3}{2}, \frac{5}{3},
\frac{7}{4}, \frac{11}{6}, 2\}$,
and $N \in \{1, \ldots, \Nmax\}$, for each $k \in \{2, \ldots, \Kmax\}$.
For every candidate triple $(k, \rho, \delta, N)$ we computed the
eigenvalues of the walk operator $U_k = S_k (I_k \otimes C(\rho, \delta))$
and evaluated the root-of-unity residual
$r_N(\rho, \delta) = \max_j |\theta_j - m_j \cdot 2\pi/N|$.

Grid points with residual below $10^{-3}$ were passed to a
refinement stage: bounded scalar minimisation of $r_N$ in $\rho$,
followed by a fine local sweep of width $10^{-8}$ with $400$ samples.
A candidate $(\rho, \delta, N)$ was accepted as a revival only if
the residual after refinement fell below $10^{-10}$ and the
independent matrix-exponentiation check
$\|U_k^N - I_{2k}\| < 10^{-10}$ also passed. Continuous families in
$\rho$ were detected separately and collapsed to a single
representative.

The search found no revival for any
$k \in \{7, 9, 11, 12, 16\}$, confirming non-existence of
full-state revivals for all $k \notin \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$
with $k \le \Kmax$ on the search grid.

The search is reproducible from the script
\texttt{exp025c\_v6.py} and its output
\texttt{exp025c-v6-output.json}, both in the accompanying repository.
\end{proof}
