\begin{theorem}[1D non-existence within the search bound]
\label{thm:1d-nonexistence}
An exhaustive search over the grid $\rho \in \{j \cdot
10^{-4} : j = 1, \ldots, 9999\}$ and $\delta/\pi \in
\{0, \tfrac{1}{20}, \ldots, \tfrac{19}{20}\}$, for revival
step count $N \le \Nmax$, finds no non-trivial full-state
revival for any $k \notin \{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10\}$ with
$k \le \Kmax$. The completeness of the 1D classification for
$k \ge 17$ and $N > \Nmax$ remains open (see
Conjecture~\ref{conj:no-other-k}).
\end{theorem}
