Full-state revivals in Discrete Time Quantum Walks (DTQW) are a fundamental property of a quantum walk on a symmetrical lattice. 
When they occur, the initial state of the walk in terms of position, spin and phase occur again after $N$ steps of a walk.
The purpose of this thesis is to find and report on revivals on tori using various parameterisable coins, examining
in detail the types of revival which can occur and when they occur for various parameteres.
